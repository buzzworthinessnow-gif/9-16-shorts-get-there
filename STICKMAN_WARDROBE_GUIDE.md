# Stickman Wardrobe Guide — Shorts 9:16

Carry forward the approved series identity: expressive white circular face, dark line-art stickman limbs, simple friendly eyes and mouth, and **light theme-appropriate clothing** against richly illustrated historical environments.

Every character prompt specifies headwear, upper garment, optional jacket/vest, trousers or shorts, footwear, and color/material. Keep the silhouette slender and readable on a phone; avoid tiny intricate costume details. Historical wardrobe follows date, place, occupation, and weather.

**Car pilot outfit:** brown flat cap, cream rolled-sleeve shirt, brown vest, charcoal cuffed trousers, and brown lace-up boots. Modern stickman host may instead wear a casual jacket, T-shirt, jeans, and sneakers. Early factory workers may wear a work cap, shirt, overalls, and boots. These are creative wardrobe references, not claims about a real individual's clothing.

**Wardrobe lock for image prompts:** “2D stickman with white round face, black slender line limbs, expressive eyes, [hat], [shirt], [jacket/vest], [trousers/shorts], [shoes], [materials/colors]. Preserve same outfit, body proportions, face, and accessories in every connected shot. No realistic anatomy or sudden wardrobe changes.”

**Motion:** Hats remain attached unless intentionally removed; sleeves and trousers move naturally; no spontaneous changes in garment colors or cuts. In 9:16 framing, show the headwear, hand gesture, and key object clearly without crowding the subject.
